Erea AI SMS Opt-In and Consent

How clients agree to receive booking texts

Erea AI sends booking text messages on behalf of participating fitness studios and other businesses that book clients into classes or time slots. Clients receive texts only after they agree.

How clients opt in by phone or in person

When a client books by phone or at the front desk, staff read this script before entering the client's mobile number:

"Would you like to receive booking confirmations and reminders by text from [business name]? Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help."

Staff enter the number only if the client says yes. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of booking.

How clients opt in online

On the business's booking page, under the name and phone fields, there is an optional checkbox. It is unchecked by default and the client must tick it themselves. Ticking it is not required to book: a client who leaves it unticked is still booked and receives no texts. The checkbox reads:

"Text me about my bookings with [business name]: confirmations, reminders and waitlist updates for sessions I ask to join. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out." followed by links to the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Below it the page says: "Optional. You can book without texts."

Erea Solo (independent instructors)

Erea Solo sends the same kinds of booking texts on behalf of independent fitness instructors (for example, Pilates and yoga instructors and personal trainers) to their own clients. Clients opt in the same two ways: online, by choosing to tick the optional, unchecked consent box described above on the instructor's booking page (booking works without it); or in person or by phone, where the instructor reads the script above before adding the client's mobile number. Each text names the instructor's business and includes STOP instructions. For example, on the booking page for the instructor business Solo Demo Pilates (ereaai.com/solo/book), the optional, unchecked consent box reads:

"Text me about my bookings with Solo Demo Pilates: confirmations, reminders and waitlist updates for sessions I ask to join. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out." followed by links to the Privacy Policy and Terms.

When that instructor books a client in person or by phone, they first ask:

"Would you like to receive booking confirmations and reminders by text from Solo Demo Pilates? Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help."

Example confirmation text:

"Hi Maya! You're booked with Solo Demo Pilates: Small Group Mat, Sat Nov 7 at 6:00 PM. Where: Verona Park, Verona, NJ 07044. Please pay your instructor before class. Reply C to confirm or R to cancel. Reply STOP to opt out."

Intake form confirmation

New clients can also confirm consent on the business's Google Forms intake form with a checkbox:

"I agree to receive class confirmations and reminders by text from [business name]. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

Messages you may receive

Booking confirmations, reminders, cancellation notices and, for clients who asked to join a waitlist, a text when a spot opens in that session. No marketing or promotional messages. Example:

"Hi Maya! You're booked at Demo Fitness: Spin, Mon Nov 2 at 6:00 PM. New here? Please fill out our 1-minute intake form before you come in: https://forms.gle/VBemTQFp7UJPe8qu7 Reply STOP to opt out."

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
