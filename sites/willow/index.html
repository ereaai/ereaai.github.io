Our story

Small on purpose

Willow started in a sunny back room with six mats and one rule: no one gets lost in the crowd.

Classes stay small so every person gets noticed, corrected and cheered on. Come as you are.

Questions

What if a class is full?

Join the waitlist when you book. If someone cancels, you get a text. Reply YES and the spot is yours.

How do I cancel?

Reply R to your reminder text, or tap cancel on your booking. Please give us 12 hours so someone on the waitlist can take your spot.

I'm new. Which class should I take?

Start with Mat Pilates. Every class has options for every level.
