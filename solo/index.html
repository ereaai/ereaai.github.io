Questions

Good to know

I teach in different places every week. Does that work?

Yes, that's what Erea Solo is built for. Each session has its own location and address, and the texts tell clients exactly where to go. Moving one week? Change it in your schedule sheet or text us and we'll update it.

Do my clients need an app?

No. They book from your link and get regular texts. If they like, they can save your link to their home screen and it opens like an app.

How do I get paid?

However you do now: Tap to Pay on your phone, Venmo, Zelle or cash. Tap "Mark paid" in My Schedule and you'll always know who still owes. Online payments when they book are coming soon as a $29 add-on.

Can I do privates and small groups?

Yes. A private is a session with 1 spot, a duet has 2, and a small group can have up to 12. Full sessions get a waitlist automatically.

How long does setup take?

We build it for you. Send us your times, places and prices, and your booking link is ready within days. Texting goes live once the phone carriers approve your number, usually about two weeks.

I also teach at a studio. Can the studio use Erea?

Yes. Studios use Erea Studio, built for front desks, staff and bigger classes. Erea Solo is for the clients you run yourself.
