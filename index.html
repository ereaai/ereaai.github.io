Questions

Good to know

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Everything happens by regular text and a booking link. If they like, they can add the link to their home screen and it opens like an app.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks, mostly waiting on the phone carriers to approve your studio's text number. You send us your schedule, prices and client list. We do the rest.

Can I keep Mindbody, Vagaro or my current software?

Yes. The Waitlist Add-on works next to what you already use and handles the cancelled-spot texting.

What about ClassPass?

ClassPass bookings keep coming through ClassPass. Erea handles your own members and drop-ins, which is where empty spots hurt most.

Will my clients get spammed?

No. They only get texts about their own bookings and open spots they asked for, and anyone can reply STOP at any time.

Is there a contract or a cut of my sales?

Neither. One flat monthly price, cancel anytime, and every dollar your clients pay goes to you.
