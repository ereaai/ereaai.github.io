How it works

Someone cancels. Erea fills it.

No new habits for you and no app for your clients. It runs on texts, your booking page and one simple screen.

1

2

Questions

Good to know

What's the difference between Erea and Erea Solo?

Erea is for any business that wants a real website, whether it's just you or a whole team. Erea Solo is for solo operators on the go who just need a booking link and one place for everything. A solo barber who wants a website picks Erea; a solo barber who just wants a link in their Instagram bio picks Erea Solo.

Do I really get my own website?

Yes. With Erea you pick one of 5 looks, and we build it with your name, logo, photos, prices and colors. You get a free address like yourname.ereaai.com. Already own a domain? We connect it free. Want one? For $5 a month we get yourname.com for you and connect it, and it stays yours if you ever leave. Your custom site is set up as part of onboarding.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Everything happens by regular text and your booking page. If they like, they can add it to their home screen and it opens like an app.

How long does setup take?

About two weeks, mostly waiting on the phone carriers to approve your business's text number. You send us your schedule, prices and client list. We do the rest.

I book with Acuity, Square, Calendly or my DMs. Can I switch?

Yes, that's exactly who Erea is for. We move your schedule, prices and client list over for you. If you're already on Mindbody, Arketa, Mariana Tek or Momence, those already refill spots, so we'll tell you honestly that you don't need us.

Will my clients get spammed?

No. They only get texts about their own bookings and open spots they asked for, and anyone can reply STOP at any time.

Is there a contract or a cut of my sales?

Neither. One flat monthly price, cancel anytime, and every dollar your clients pay goes to you.
